FAQ

Business setup in Bahrain: frequently asked questions

How can I start a business in Bahrain?

Starting a business in Bahrain typically involves choosing your business activity, selecting a legal structure (such as a W.L.L. or Single Person Company), reserving a trade name, preparing incorporation documents, securing a registered address, and applying for a Commercial Registration (CR) through the Ministry of Industry and Commerce's Sijilat portal. Some activities also need approvals from other authorities. A consultant can manage these steps for you.

How much does it cost to set up a business in Bahrain?

There is no single fixed price. The total cost depends on your business activity, company type, office space, number of visas, government fees and professional service fees. Request a personalised quotation and we will give you a clear, itemised estimate for your specific case.

Can foreigners own 100% of a company in Bahrain?

Bahrain permits 100% foreign ownership for many commercial activities. However, some activities remain restricted or require a Bahraini or GCC partner. Ownership rules depend on the specific activity and current regulations, so we confirm eligibility for your activity before you commit.

How long does company registration take in Bahrain?

Timelines vary. Straightforward registrations can be completed relatively quickly once documents are ready, while activities that need additional ministry approvals or licences take longer. We give you a realistic timeline after reviewing your activity and documents.

What documents are required to register a company in Bahrain?

Commonly required documents include passport copies of shareholders and directors, proof of address, a memorandum of association, a lease agreement for the business premises, and — for corporate shareholders — certified and attested company documents. Exact requirements depend on the company type and activity.

What is a Commercial Registration (CR) in Bahrain?

A Commercial Registration (CR) is the official record issued by the Ministry of Industry and Commerce that legally establishes your business and lists its approved activities, owners and address. You need a valid CR to trade, open a corporate bank account and apply for visas.

Can a foreigner open a business in Bahrain?

Yes. Foreign individuals and companies can open a business in Bahrain, either as a new company or as a branch of an existing foreign company. The available structures and ownership share depend on the business activity.

What are the different types of companies in Bahrain?

Common legal forms include the With Limited Liability company (W.L.L.), Single Person Company (S.P.C.), Bahraini Shareholding Company (B.S.C., closed or public), branch of a foreign company, and partnership structures. Sole proprietorship is generally available to Bahraini and GCC nationals. We help you choose the right structure.

Do I need a physical office to start a business in Bahrain?

Most companies need a registered business address supported by a lease agreement. The type and size of premises required can depend on the activity, and some activities may qualify for smaller or shared office options. We advise on the most cost-effective compliant option.

Can I get an investor visa through company formation?

Company owners and partners may be eligible for residence permits linked to their business, and Bahrain also offers other residency routes such as the Golden Residency Visa for those who meet specific criteria. Eligibility depends on your circumstances and current immigration rules.

This information is general guidance, not legal advice. Requirements change — we confirm the current rules for your case.
